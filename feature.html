Cloud technology gives teams the freedom to build, deploy and scale digital products without being limited by physical infrastructure. It brings secure computing power, storage and intelligent services closer to the people who need them.

Adapt resources to real demand. Cloud platforms can expand during busy moments and streamline when traffic settles.
